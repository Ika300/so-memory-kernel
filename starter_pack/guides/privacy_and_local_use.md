# Local Privacy Guide

This pack is designed for local-first work.

## Recommended privacy posture

- Keep client files on your own machine.
- Do not upload raw client traces to external AI services.
- Do not paste confidential retrieved text into ChatGPT, Groq, Claude, Gemini, or other cloud systems without written client approval.
- Use anonymized examples when preparing public demos.
- Remove company names, personal names, source IDs, dates, and direct quotations before sharing anything publicly.

## What can usually be shared safely after review

Only after manual checking:

- structural observations
- counts
- anonymized evidence patterns
- generic failure categories
- rewritten example snippets

## What should not be shared by default

- raw retrieved text
- customer names
- employee names
- document IDs that identify internal systems
- exact dates
- proprietary policy text
- confidential product or support content

## Suggested client explanation

"The analysis can be performed locally. The report focuses on evidence structure, such as repeated-source reliance and independent support. Raw customer data does not need to be sent to an external AI service."
